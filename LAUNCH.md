**Title:** I made a free daily Worlds game: guess the mystery pro in 8 tries, plus pick'em with live scores

Hey all. For Swiss I built a free fan site with three parts:

- **Scout Daily:** a new mystery Worlds pro every day. Each guess compares team, role, region, nation, rating, KDA and signature champ. After every two misses you unlock another hint. Your solve speed gives you a "scout rank", from Challenger at 1 guess down to Iron.
- **Today:** pick every match and keep a streak. Scores update live, and the Swiss standings and your record against an Elo model update as games end.
- **Face-off:** pick any two pros or teams and compare their 2026 stats, champion pools, head-to-heads and series odds.

No login and no paywall. Stats come from gol.gg and lolesports. Feedback welcome, and post your Scout Daily results below.
